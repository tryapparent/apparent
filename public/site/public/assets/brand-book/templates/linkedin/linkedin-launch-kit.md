2. Create a Company Page from an authenticated founder profile.
3. Use `tryapparent` as the public URL.
4. Upload the approved 400px logo and 4200 by 700px cover.
5. Paste the approved tagline and About copy.
6. Add `https://tryapparent.com` as the website.
7. Add the truthful company size and headquarters.
8. Assign both founders as admins.
9. Link each founder experience entry to the company page.
10. Review the result on desktop and mobile before publishing a first post.

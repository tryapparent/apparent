# Archived Apparent Pi demo cut

The former landing page video, `public/apparent-demo-pi.mp4` (21.3 s, 1200×720 H.264, **no audio stream**), and its poster are no longer included in the public site. The video was a browser recording of the interactive demo directly beneath it, not a live Pi session. The former interactive demo is also no longer the landing-page demonstration; the current site uses `src/demo/VideoDemo.tsx`. The retired iPhone demo and its narration assets have been removed. Desktop narration remains in `public/apparent-narration-mac.mp3`; its word onsets and caption text are in `src/demo/script.ts`.

The narration was generated locally with Piper’s `en_GB-cori-medium` voice. The voice’s [model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/raw/main/en/en_GB/cori/medium/MODEL_CARD) identifies the source dataset as public-domain LibriVox recordings. Mac lines: “The live badge overlaps the title.” / “And the buttons sit too low. Move the badge and align the row.” The historical iPhone narration described a title and a clipped action bar; it is not a current support claim. Each first line starts 1.1 s after capture begins; the second starts at 5.4 s. The two 10 s tracks were encoded with FFmpeg and are distinct from the previous ElevenLabs narration.

The screen recording was made with T3 Code’s collaborative browser recording of the local production build. The interactive demo was played from t=0; the browser capture was cropped to its 1200×720 stage and trimmed to 21.3 s. The recording hid only the interactive player’s sound hint and replay overlay. The first cut mixed in the new Mac narration 3.6 s into the video; Simon rejected the voice, so the MP4 was remuxed with `-map 0:v:0 -c:v copy -an` to remove its audio stream without re-encoding the picture.

Build with `APPARENT_PUBLIC_SITE=1 bun run build && node scripts/package-apparent.mjs` from `site/`. The allowlisted `dist-apparent` artifact omits the retired video and poster, previous narration, Apparent releases, the update feed, and the feature map.

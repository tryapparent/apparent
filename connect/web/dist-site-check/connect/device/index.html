Upload details

Captures can include screen images, recording, voice, transcript and window context.

This computer’s grant can add, read and delete uploaded captures. Agent read approval is separate.

Google sign-in alone grants no upload or read permission. A capture ID is not a public sharing link.

Retention & limits
  • Seven days maximum. The 500 MB (500,000,000-byte) account cap evicts oldest captures first, possibly earlier. Keep a local copy.
  • Any file over 90 MB (90,000,000 bytes) fails the whole upload. Complete bundles only.
  • Free during beta, with at most 20 accounts.
  • Grants expire after 30 days, with no automatic renewal. Reapprove through the CLI; website sign-in does not renew grants.
